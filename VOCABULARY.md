## Usage Notes
- "Takeaways" is common in business English for the key points or conclusions from a discussion.
- "Conclusions" is more formal and refers to judgements reached after considering information.
- "Lessons learned" focuses on what experience has taught us.
- Use "last" for duration: "The meeting lasted an hour."
- Use "meet" for arranging or having a meeting with people; the past form is "met".
- "Fall behind" is the correct phrase — not "hold behind".
- "Heard of" is more natural than "heard about" for knowing that something exists.
- For dates: use "on" with a specific day (on February fourth) and "in" with a month or year (in February, in 2027).
- "Chest" is the safe everyday word; "breast" has a different, more specific meaning.
- "To be into something" is informal but very common in spoken English.
