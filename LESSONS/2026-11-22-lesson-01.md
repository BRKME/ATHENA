## Useful Expressions
- Take your time.
- Go on, I'm listening.
- To be honest...
- I'm off.
- By the way...
- Keep in mind...
- It lasted more than three hours.
- We walked around the neighbourhood.
- We met our friends at a bar.

## Key Takeaways
1. Use "some" with uncountable nouns in phrases such as "some free time" and "some water".
2. "Manage stock levels" is the natural business collocation.
3. "Throughout my career" is more idiomatic than "through my career".
4. "Takeaways", "conclusions" and "lessons learned" overlap but are not interchangeable in every context.
5. Use the past simple to describe completed events: "we walked", "we met", "I had".
6. Use "lasted" to describe the duration of an event: "The play lasted more than three hours."
7. The tutor must wait until the learner has clearly finished before correcting or asking the next question.
