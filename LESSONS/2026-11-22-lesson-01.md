## Corrections and Better Phrasing
| Original / issue | More natural English |
|---|---|
| It was a nice show, the hall was full | It was a nice show. The hall was full. |
| I have a free time | I had some free time. |
| I visited a restaurant in the hall | During the break at the theatre, I went to a restaurant. |
| There were not huge queue | There wasn't a long queue. |
| I have bought a game and played it | I played an Xbox game, Atomic Heart, with my son. |
| I play in Russian | I played in Russian. |
| I owned stock level | I manage stock levels. |
| Through my career | Throughout my career |
| customer relationship | customer relationships |
| don't be so quickly | Please speak more slowly / Please don't rush. |
| выводы | takeaways / conclusions / lessons learned |
| устойчивые фразеологизмы | idiomatic expressions / fixed phrases |

## Useful Expressions
- Take your time.
- Go on, I'm listening.
- To be honest...
- I'm off.
- By the way...
- Keep in mind...

## Key Takeaways
1. Use "some" with uncountable nouns in phrases such as "some free time" and "some water".
2. "Manage stock levels" is the natural business collocation.
3. "Throughout my career" is more idiomatic than "through my career".
4. "Takeaways", "conclusions" and "lessons learned" overlap but are not interchangeable in every context.
5. The tutor must wait until the learner has clearly finished before correcting or asking the next question.
