### Priorities
1. Articles ("a", "an", "the") and countable/uncountable nouns.
2. Past simple forms and tense consistency when telling stories.
3. Natural collocations and idiomatic expressions.
4. Sequencing events with "first", "then", "after that" and "finally".
5. Expanding answers beyond short sentences while maintaining clarity.
6. Tutor must leave enough silence and never interrupt the learner.

## Latest Lesson Takeaways — 2026-11-22

- "Walk around the neighbourhood" is a natural phrase for a casual stroll.
